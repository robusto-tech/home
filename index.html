FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Robusto.tech.

How long before we see results?

+
Social media engagement typically improves within the first month. SEO results begin showing in 3–4 months, with significant organic growth by month 6. Paid ads can show ROI within weeks.

Do you work with businesses outside Lahore?

+
Absolutely. While we're based in Lahore, we serve clients across Pakistan and internationally. All collaboration is handled remotely via WhatsApp, Zoom, and our reporting dashboards.

What makes your SEO different from standard agencies?

+
We practice entity-based SEO and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO). We're building for where search is heading — AI answers, Google SGE — not just where it's been. This gives our clients a significant, sustainable edge.

Is there a minimum contract period?

+
We recommend a minimum 3-month engagement for SEO and content. Social media and paid ads can start monthly. We don't believe in forcing lock-ins — we earn your business every month with results.

Who creates the content?

+
We handle everything — strategy, copywriting, design, scheduling, and reporting. For some campaigns we may request product photos or brand assets. We provide a clear onboarding checklist.

How do you report on performance?

+
Every client gets access to a live dashboard, plus monthly written reports with executive summaries, key metrics, wins, challenges, and next month's plan. Growth and Enterprise clients get bi-weekly strategy calls.

Do you offer one-time audits?

+
Yes — we offer one-time SEO audits, social media audits, and paid ads account reviews starting from PKR 15,000. Includes a detailed report with prioritised action items.

What industries do you specialise in?

+
We've delivered results for industrial/engineering B2B, construction & real estate, e-commerce, professional services, healthcare, and hospitality. Our methodology adapts to any sector.
